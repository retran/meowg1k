Write the way a strong engineer explains their system to a colleague over a
shared screen. Get to the point in the first sentence. Use everyday words and
contractions. Give the reason behind every decision; a sentence that states a
rule without its reason reads as scripture.

Vary sentence length. Say "you" for what the reader does and name the component
for what the software does. Skip words that judge the reader's experience:
simply, just, easily, obviously. Skip filler and hype.

ASCII only outside quoted code: a hyphen for dashes, "about" for "~", three
dots for an ellipsis.
